# Front-Service Plant Watering Enclosure V2

[Download on Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1912984-front-service-plant-watering-enclosure-v2)

A mechanical prototype for a four-zone plant watering system. The rounded reservoir and solar lid sit behind a front-service pod. A removable electronics cassette and battery drawer occupy the center, with separate valve trays and covers on either side and a wet pump drawer below. The archive includes the 30 individual printed parts in STL and STEP, build dimensions and service notes.

Start with the separate fit-coupon pack. Test your printer, inserts and glands before committing to the main shells. Use the print-oriented STL files. The reservoir footprint nearly fills a 290 mm circle before adding any brim, and several other pieces are 300 mm tall. Check the actual build area, the first layer, supports and tool access for every part in your slicer.

Orion verified that the individual meshes are closed and checked nominal drawer clearances and sampled service paths. The installed and exploded views show the intended arrangement, including hardware envelopes. Printed fit, leakage, outdoor use and autonomous operation remain untested. Use this package for mechanical fit development.
