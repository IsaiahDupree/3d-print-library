# Curved Wall Bracket V2

[Download on Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1912980-curved-wall-bracket-v2)

A prototype bracket with a two-hole backplate and a curved lip. The original Cypress project calls it Dust Devil Mount. Its exact mating item and mounting fasteners were not specified in the files.

The STL spans approximately 167.4 × 29.3 × 66.9 mm in the export axes. Inspect the curved contact area and hole spacing in the STEP and compare both to the item you intend to fit. Check support under the lip in your slicer.

Orion verified one closed STL mesh and a matching solid STEP. Load capacity and actual wall or tool fit remain unverified. Use the first print for a stationary, unloaded fit check.
