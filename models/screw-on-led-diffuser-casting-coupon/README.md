# Screw-On LED Diffuser and Silicone Mold Test Kit

[Download on Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1912972-screw-on-led-diffuser-and-silicone-mold-test-kit)

A small bench model for trying a screw-on translucent face before committing to a full illuminated sign. It includes the LED pocket backing, a casting mold with hole pins, the nominal face shape and two retaining washers. The face is 2 mm thick in CAD and the two holes sit opposite each other on a 24 mm center-to-center line.

Print the backing, washers and mold as separate parts. The face file gives the intended casting shape and can also be used for a translucent print trial. Check screw engagement, mold release, hole tearing and compression on the cooled coupon. The modeled compression target is 0.2 mm. Measure the actual face and stops before tightening.

The exploded render shows where a narrow LED strip sits beneath the face. Test brightness, color and temperature on the bench before scaling the design. Orion checked the exported solids. Silicone casting, print fit and optical or thermal performance remain untested.
