# Scraper Holder for FLSUN V400

[Download on Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1912976-scraper-holder-for-flsun-v400)

A two-screw holder with a slotted pocket for a scraper. V1 has a compact body. V2 is wider and has a relieved opening beneath the upright so the scraper can be grabbed from above.

The compact version measures approximately 44.1 × 25.8 × 45.9 mm. The wider version measures 49.2 × 37.8 × 45.9 mm. Use the STEP files to check the scraper opening and mounting pattern against your tool and the intended location on the printer.

Keep the holder and scraper clear of the printer's moving parts. Preview the slot and mounting holes in your slicer. Orion verified both meshes as closed solids. Actual fasteners, orientation and fit remain unverified.
