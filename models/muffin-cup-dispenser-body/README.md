# Muffin Cup Dispenser — Rack Body Prototype

[Download on Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1912762-muffin-cup-dispenser-rack-body-prototype)

A lattice rack with a curved outlet for testing a stack of muffin cups or liners. This is the rack body from the original DesignModeler project, recovered from the Cypress archive. The model includes the lattice and the curved delivery section as one piece.

The STL measures 315.7 × 148.0 × 90.5 mm along the exported X, Y and Z axes. Check that your slicer can fit the part on the actual build surface with room for supports and adhesion. Preview the lattice and outlet layer by layer before starting a print. The editable STEP is in the ZIP.

The source drawing used tapered envelope solids to represent cups. The mouth of each modeled envelope is about 93 mm across. Measure your own liners and check feed clearance on the printed rack. The render shows the released rack geometry. Cup envelopes and the separate experimental braces are outside this download.

Orion verified the rack as one closed STL and reopened the matching STEP as a valid solid. Print orientation, supports and physical feed have yet to be tested. This is a dimensional and feed prototype.
