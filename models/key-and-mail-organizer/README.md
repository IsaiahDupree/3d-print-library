# Wall-Mounted Key and Mail Organizer

[Download on Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1912698-wall-mounted-key-and-mail-organizer)

Designed by Isaiah Dupree. The wall panel combines key hooks and angled mail pockets in one printable piece.

The archived STL measures about 356 × 229 × 95 mm in its model axes. Lay the broad back against the build plate in your slicer. Check brim and bed clearance, plus the hooks and pockets for any overhangs that need support. If your bed is too small, split the part and plan the joints before printing.

Orion checked the recovered STL and found one closed mesh. The cover shows the recovered geometry. No completed print, mounting method, or load limit was verified for this release. Use it for light household items and choose mounting hardware for your wall.
