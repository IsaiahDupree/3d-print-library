# Ordered 3/8-Inch Metric Socket Organizer

[Download on Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1912774-ordered-38-inch-metric-socket-organizer)

Five lift-out modules hold the 24 shallow and deep 3/8-inch-drive metric sockets in the TEKTON SHD91215 size range. Each pocket is labeled. The shallow sockets sit in front of the deep sockets, in ascending 8–19 mm order. Two longer holders organize the compact ratchet and extensions.

The ZIP has separate STEP and STL sets for the DWST17805 and DWST08165 layouts. Use one configuration as a set. The direct STL downloads are for DWST17805. The DWST17805 configuration includes the SRH11503 ratchet and SHA11106 extension. DWST08165 adds the SHA11103 extension and has wider pocket clearances. The table in the ZIP identifies each accessory holder.

Print the 30 × 30 mm coupons first and try the actual 10 mm shallow socket. The three versions give 0.25, 0.50 and 0.75 mm clearance per side around the catalog outside diameter. Mark each coupon as it comes off the bed. Print the holders flat, pockets facing up, and check the floor, labels and brim in your slicer. The long modules require more bed length than the socket blocks.

The preview shows the holders inside the project's open trays. Those trays are a separate design. The holders rely on gravity and are intended for upright storage. CAD clearance and removal checks passed. Actual socket fit, hand access and closed-lid clearance remain unverified.
