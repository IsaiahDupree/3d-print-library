# Modular Open Toolbox Trays — 42 mm Pitch

[Download on Printables](https://www.printables.com/model/1912741-modular-open-toolbox-trays-42-mm-pitch)

Eight open rectangular tray sizes for organizing small hardware and tools in a drawer. Lay out the trays on a 42 mm pitch with a nominal 1 mm gap between adjacent trays.

Sizes range from a 41 × 41 mm square to a 251 × 41 mm long tray. All versions are 20 mm tall with 2 mm walls and a 2 mm floor. The ZIP contains editable STEP and STL files for every size and the geometry source notes.

Print the tray flat on its base. Measure the usable drawer floor, including rounded corners and ribs, and check the clearance above the trays. Start with the 1 × 1 tray to verify your print dimensions. The 6 × 1 tray needs at least 251 mm of usable bed length before allowing for a brim or skirt.

FreeCAD checked the exported dimensions and solid geometry. Physical fit and load capacity remain unverified. These trays sit loose on a flat surface and have no interlocking base.
