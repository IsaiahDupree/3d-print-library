# Contributing and requests

This is Isaiah Dupree's release mirror rather than an open upload directory. Please use the design-request issue form for new ideas, corrections, fitment reports, or requests for a variation.

Do not submit confidential drawings, private addresses, credentials, vehicle identifiers, licence plates, or personal information. By sharing measurements or media, confirm that you have permission to share them for evaluation.

Useful feedback identifies the model and revision, printer, nozzle, material, settings, measured result, and clear evidence of the issue.
